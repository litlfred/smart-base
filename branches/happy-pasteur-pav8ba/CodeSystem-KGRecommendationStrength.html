CodeSystem: Knowledge graph L1: recommendation strength

Official URL: http://smart.who.int/base/CodeSystem/KGRecommendationStrength Version: 0.3.0
Active as of 2026-10-07 Computable Name: KGRecommendationStrength

GRADE strength. Strength is not certainty: a strong recommendation can rest on low-certainty evidence and a conditional one on high. Record "weak" as conditional; the handbook treats them as synonyms (10.4). Source: smart-kg ontology/l1/l1.json valueSets "recommendation-strength"; WHO handbook for guideline development (2014) 10.4). CodeDisplayDefinition
