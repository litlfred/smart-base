L1/schemas/ l1-schemas

Knowledge-graph layer L1, version 3.0, as Zod: what a WHO publication says. Its publication, section and element layout; the recommendations, remarks, key questions, outcomes, evidence, interventions and indicators it prints; and the citations a DAK uses to point at them. `l1.ts` is the layer as migrated from WHO smart-kg 3f5e477, and `l1-library.ts` is the library extension (L1's layout classes specialise the ingested library's, plus `implementation-practice`). It also holds the engine every layer uses: `ontology.ts` (the layer shape), `props.ts`, `vocab.ts`, `graph.ts` (the graph-document shape), `context.ts` and `loader.ts` (JSON-LD), `fsh.ts` and `emit.ts` (generation), `validate.ts` and `kgid.ts`, with `package.json` for `kg.yml`. `generated/` holds the ontology JSON, the document JSON Schema and the JSON-LD contexts; the FSH goes to the IG's `input/fsh/`.

L2/schemas/ l2-schemas

Knowledge-graph layer L2, version 3.0: the Digital Adaptation Kit as nine components (DAK.fsh) and the elements inside them: the kit, personas, user scenarios, business processes and their tasks, data elements, decision-support and scheduling logic, programme indicators, functional and non-functional requirements with their statements, test scenarios, and the external artefacts they point at. Imports L1, and licenses the L1 -> L2 edges L1 cannot declare itself. `l2.ts` is the layer as migrated from WHO smart-kg 3f5e477, typed; `index.ts` exports it to the engine in `../../L1/schemas/`, and `generated/` holds the ontology JSON and the JSON-LD context. The FSH goes to the IG's `input/fsh/`.
